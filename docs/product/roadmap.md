# Roadmap

| Phase | Features | Must prove before moving on | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | 105 | Sellers connect and run confirmation within a day; confirmation and delivery rates rise in pilot stores; trials convert to paid | Growth spending and phase 2 |
| **Growth** | 107 | Sellers stay month after month; AI and flows handle a large share of chats; app listings and partners bring signups | The Gulf and agencies |
| **Scale** | 30 | Larger teams adopt without custom work; partners resell; service targets hold through peak seasons | New markets, larger accounts |

The full feature list with phases is in `Product-Feature-Specification.pdf` and the appendix of the brand book.
