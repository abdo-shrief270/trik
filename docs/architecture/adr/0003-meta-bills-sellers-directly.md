# ADR 0003: Meta bills sellers directly

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25

## Context

WhatsApp message fees can be resold through a prepaid wallet or billed by Meta to the seller.

## Decision

Trik is a Meta Tech Provider. Sellers add their own payment method in WhatsApp Manager and Meta bills them directly. Trik holds no wallet and adds no markup. Trik shows costs before sending and enforces budget caps.

## Consequences

Simpler finance and compliance, transparent pricing. Trik's revenue is the subscription only.
