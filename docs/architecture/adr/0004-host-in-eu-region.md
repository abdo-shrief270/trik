# ADR 0004: Host in an EU region

- **Status:** Accepted · refined by [ADR 0011](0011-hostinger-first-aws-later.md) (Hostinger EU first, AWS later)
- **Date:** 2026-11-25

## Context

Customers are in Egypt and the Gulf. Local hosting options are limited.

## Decision

Production runs in a European cloud region close to Egypt. The cross-border transfer permit under Egypt's data protection law is a launch requirement.

## Consequences

Mature cloud services and low latency to Cairo. A permit and transfer safeguards are required.
