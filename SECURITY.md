# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@trik.chat** with the details and steps to reproduce. Do not open a public issue.
We acknowledge within 2 business days and agree on a fix timeline with you.

## Rules for this repository

- No secrets, tokens, customer data or production dumps in the repository, issues or pull requests.
- Customer phone numbers and messages are personal data under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020) and similar laws in the Gulf. Use synthetic data in tests and seeds.
- WhatsApp data is used only for the seller who owns it, as Meta's terms require.
- Dependencies are scanned in CI. High and critical findings block merges.
